SPIFFE Token Exchange — CEO Identity Descoping

🔊

What this demonstrates

A bank CEO delegates their identity to an AI agent in an OpenShell sandbox. The agent can read bank data but cannot write — even though the CEO has full permissions. Identity preserved via SPIFFE token exchange, permissions reduced by the provider profile.
